What's secretly holding you back?

Three quick questions. Choose your answer and see what Hellen Chen says.

  1. When choosing a partner, what is your biggest priority?

    Hellen says: Choosing on looks and surface conditions is a common trap. Look for someone who shares your direction and is willing to grow.

  2. How do you approach responsibility in a relationship?

    Hellen says: Meeting halfway often leads to 100% failure in marriage. Real success comes when you take 200% responsibility.

  3. What is your view on dating before marriage?

    Hellen says: Dating blindly for years often brings friction and wasted time. Choose a committed partner, step into marriage, and nurture romance for a lifetime.
