The 10 lessons

Open any lesson to read the key takeaway.

Lesson 1: Do You Have to Win at All Cost?

Winning at all costs creates resentment and damages relationships. True happiness comes from knowing when to compete, and taking both winning and losing in stride.

Lesson 2: Is It Good to Be Selfish?

Unchecked selfishness leads to friction and arguments. When you help others reach their goals, harmony follows and your own life becomes smoother.

Lesson 3: Living a Fun and Interesting Life

Boredom comes from unresolved issues and unfinished tasks. Rest well, be honest, and finish what you start, and life becomes energetic and fun.

Lesson 4: Having Your Full Confidence in Place

True confidence is not ego. It comes from knowing your real level of performance, mastering the basics, and refusing to give up.

Lesson 5: Improving Your Interpersonal Relationships

Good relationships require holding back unnecessary criticism and practicing two-way communication, so both people feel heard and respected.

Lesson 6: Stimulate the Creativity in Your Life

Routine without innovation ages the mind. Bring your personal strengths into daily habits to keep life exciting.

Lesson 7: Diploma vs. Ability

Credentials may open doors, but practical ability and an understanding of people sustain a career.

Lesson 8: My Weak Points

Ignoring weak points leaves you vulnerable. Identify them and practice until they become strengths.

Lesson 9: Increase Your Energy on Performing Work

Treat work as an engaging game rather than a burden. Getting fully into the motion of a task unlocks energy and creativity.

Lesson 10: Break Through Your Limitations

Life brings collisions and bottlenecks. Facing root issues directly and building self-discipline lets you break through to new stages of growth.
